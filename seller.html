THE ART OF HERBAL OIL MAKING

Slow process.
Pure ingredients.
Natural results.

Every bottle begins with carefully selected herbs. From preparation to infusion and filtration, each stage is handled with patience and attention to preserve the character of every botanical ingredient.

FROM HERB TO BOTTLE

The Making

Discover the traditional process behind our herbal oils, where carefully selected botanicals are prepared and infused with quality carrier oils.

01
Step 01 · Selection

Collecting the Herbs

We begin by selecting suitable herbs and removing damaged or unwanted plant material. Careful selection helps create a clean and consistent starting point for the infusion process.

02
Step 02 · Cleaning

Clean with care

The herbs are cleaned and thoroughly dried before infusion. Removing excess moisture is an important part of preparing plant material for oil-based extraction.

03
Step 03 · Preparation

Prepare & Infuse

The dried herbs are gently cut or crushed and combined with a suitable carrier oil. During the infusion period, oil-soluble components from the botanicals gradually transfer into the carrier oil.

04
Step 04 · Infusion

Strain & Filter

Once the infusion is complete, the plant material is carefully separated from the oil. The oil is filtered to remove remaining botanical particles.

05
Step 05 · Bottle & Store

Develop the blend

The finished oil is transferred into clean, dry containers and stored away from excessive heat, moisture and direct sunlight to help maintain its quality.
