# 🌿SIVA & HERBOIL — Natural Oils & Herbal Care
Project Link https://ecommerceoil.netlify.app/

# HERBOIL** is a modern and responsive herbal oil website designed to showcase natural oils, traditional ingredients, and everyday hair and body care products through a clean and attractive online experience.

## ✨ About the Project
<img width="1920" height="5027" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5322ecf7-2b01-4543-80d5-6cc987d1d97e" />

HERBOIL brings the idea of **traditional herbal care into a modern digital storefront**. The website focuses on natural ingredients, simple product discovery, and a smooth shopping experience.

From product highlights to detailed product information, every section is designed to create a warm, natural, and trustworthy brand experience.

## 🎯 Project Goals

* Create a clean and attractive herbal-oil website
* Present products in an easy-to-browse layout
* Highlight natural ingredients and traditional care
* Provide responsive design for mobile, tablet, and desktop
* Create a simple and user-friendly shopping interface
* Build a strong visual identity for the HERBOIL brand
